The approved design prototype of Umpire's Eye, kept as the design reference: the parity and speed tests load it next to the app, in the same browser engine, and compare against it. It isn't part of the app, and it reads its images and fonts from `art/`. Its text follows the app's on purpose where the app's words changed: the World Series' ≈ 94% shows only in the hook and on the card, not in the score chip, the verdict or the card back's foot.
